# Hello World:

This is just an introduction blog post that I wrote to both introduce my blog and also to test to make sure it works properly.

I wanted to add a blog that was simple on paper: a folder with files in markdown that are than loaded onto the blog webpage for navigation.

I did learn that GitHub Pages does have Jekyll built in which did nearly all the heavy lifting for me and I just needed to do something simple
to get the blog pages to be displayed in the format that I wanted.

For more information on Jekyll, here is the documentation for posts: https://jekyllrb.com/docs/posts/

----

The goal of this blog is to have my own little corner of the internet to write whatever I want without the noise of the internet especially social media
and the judgement that comes from other people.

The idea is to also present ideas and thoughts that are my own even if other people may disagree with them.

----

### A little about me:

I have a webpage called "About Me" on this site, but since this is an introductory blog post, I'm going to give a quick rundown:

My name is Alex which is not my real name, but what I tend to go by online due to it being gender neutral and also for more personal reasons.

I have an interest in computers, but also in stuff such as OSINT and gathering data to the point it might be unhealthy. I also have dabbled in 
computer hardware related things, lockpicking, collecting pens and pencils, and a lot more.

I have very little goals these days due to the current state of this floating rock, but I will try my best to post and share things that I want to share
since I believe that even if only one or two or a handful of people see or find what I posted helpful, that is more than enough, and I believe that
most things such as software and the knowledge that comes with it, should be easily accessible to anyone and I dislike the gatekeeping that I've seen
get worse in the past several years.

I do program, but I despise programming with a passion and I don't consider myself a very good programmer. 

I write scripts and programs to help me with various tasks that are usually for automation and to help me gather data in large quantities. I have a public 
github which I use to post tools and scripts that I wrote, in hopes that it would help someone else, but not really a priority since I have no plans on 
ever being an OSS dev mostly because I have seen how people treat them.

I also don't believe programming is a good measurement of skills especially in fields such as information security since the field is quite large and
the toxicity that comes with comparing people to other people is something that I've grown out of a long time ago.
